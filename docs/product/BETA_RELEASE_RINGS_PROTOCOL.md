# AXIOM Beta Release Rings Protocol & Decision Governance

## 1. Authority & Strategic Objective

This document defines the operational protocol for executing Phase C (**Gate G4 Instrumented Beta**) per the AXIOM Upgrade Master Plan and Canonical Roadmap.

The primary mission of the Instrumented Beta is to **measure product truth without noise or inflation**:
- Prove that users achieve meaningful progress in their lives via AXIOM.
- Verify that First-Win and Core Loop mechanics retain users without artificial interventions.
- Strictly prohibit vanity rewards or artificial streak inflation when core retention signals are weak.

---

## 2. Canonical Release Rings

| Ring | Cohort Size | Minimum Duration | Core Validation Focus | Advancement Exit Threshold |
|---|---:|---:|---|---|
| **1. INTERNAL** | 3–5 users | 3 days | Fresh install, process death recovery, wording, zero S1 | Zero S1 crashes, clean installation/onboarding |
| **2. ALPHA** | 8–12 users | 7 days | First-Win usability, unassisted completion, TTFV | FMC $\ge 80\%$, TTFV $< 3$ min, zero S1 |
| **3. CONCIERGE** | 20–30 users | 21 days | Problem/value truth with semi-manual support | D1 $\ge 30\%$, D7 $\ge 20\%$, qualitative problem fit |
| **4. CLOSED BETA** | 50–100 users | 30–60 days | Natural retention without manual intervention | D1 $\ge 30\%$, D7 $\ge 20–25\%$, WMPU $\ge 25\%$, zero S1 |
| **5. PRODUCTION** | 100+ users | 60+ days | Scaled adoption post-G6 monetization proof | Sustainable unit economics, AI cost $< 15\%$ |

---

## 3. Privacy & Telemetry Guardrails

1. **Zero PII & Free-Text Invariant:**
   - Under no circumstances may Goal titles, Mission descriptions, Reflection/Journal text, Prompts, or Health/Finance content be transmitted or persisted in analytics event payloads.
   - All telemetry payloads must pass `AnalyticsPayloadPolicy.ALLOWLIST`.
2. **Assignment vs. Exposure Separation:**
   - Funnel and experimentation variants must emit distinct assignment events (`first_win_assigned`, `experiment_assigned`) and exposure events (`first_win_exposed`, `experiment_exposed`).
3. **Three-State Consent Compliance:**
   - All telemetry collection respects WP-206 user consent (`UNKNOWN` = retain locally with upload 0, `DECLINED` = purge local queue & drop, `GRANTED` = upload).

---

## 4. Weekly Decision Review Protocol

### Cadence & Rhythm
Every week during the beta period, the Product Owner and core maintainer convene for the **Weekly Decision Review**:
1. Review automated snapshot generated by `DecisionDashboardAggregator` and `WeeklyDecisionReviewEngine`.
2. Evaluate Gate G4 exit criteria:
   - **D1 Retention:** Target $\ge 30\%$
   - **D7 Retention:** Target $\ge 20–25\%$
   - **WMPU Rate:** Target $\ge 25\%$ (stretch 40%)
   - **S1 Defects:** Strictly 0
3. Inspect unassisted First-Win completion logs and crash reports.

### The Anti-Inflation Rule
> **Canonical Invariant:** If retention or WMPU thresholds fail during beta reviews, it is **strictly forbidden** to mask the problem by adding extra XP, free streak shields, random reward bonuses, or spam notifications. The team must formulate the **smallest vertical repair or pivot decision** addressing the real-world friction.

---

## 5. Transition to Phase D (Gate G5)

Transition from Gate G4 to Gate G5 (Retention Proof) is authorized only when:
1. Closed Beta cohort ($\ge 50$ users) completes at least 21 days of observation.
2. D1 $\ge 30\%$, D7 $\ge 20\%$, and WMPU Rate $\ge 25\%$ are confirmed with $\ge 95\%$ event completeness.
3. Zero unresolved S1 crashes exist.
4. Composite Gate score $\ge 9.50$ with zero active Hard Caps.
