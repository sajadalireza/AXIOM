VALIDATE only.

Advisory intelligence layer.

Every slice requires EN/FA, RTL, TalkBack, 200% font, >=48dp targets, reduced motion, dark/light themes, rollback, runtime evidence, four reviews, score >=9.5, and 0 Hard Caps.
